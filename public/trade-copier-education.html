Understanding Trade-Copier Technology

This page is educational only. It does not offer, recommend, or configure any copier service.

What Is Trade-Copier Technology?

Trade-copier technology is software that replicates trade activity from one account to one or more other accounts, typically to keep positions aligned across accounts.

Leader and Follower Account Structure

A "leader" account generates trade activity; "follower" accounts attempt to mirror that activity, often at a proportional size.

  • 01

    How Orders May Be Replicated

    Orders are typically detected on the leader account and re-sent to follower accounts, subject to each platform's execution logic and latency.

  • 02

    Compatible Account Considerations

    Not every broker, platform, or account type supports copier connections — compatibility should always be confirmed directly with the provider.

  • 03

    Slippage & Execution Differences

    Fills on follower accounts can differ from the leader account due to timing, liquidity, and market conditions at the moment of replication.

  • 04

    Connectivity & Platform Risks

    Internet interruptions, platform outages, or software issues can delay or prevent trade replication entirely.

  • 05

    Account Rule Differences

    Leverage, margin, and risk rules can differ between accounts, which can change how an identical trade behaves on each one.

  • 06

    Risk Settings & Position Multipliers

    Some copier tools allow scaling trade size up or down between accounts — a setting that changes risk exposure and should be fully understood before use.

Important Notices

  • Plan The Trade does not guarantee identical fills or outcomes between accounts.
  • Past results do not predict future results.
  • Technology can experience interruptions or delays.
  • Users remain fully responsible for their risk decisions to join.
  • Some brokers or prop firms may restrict or prohibit copier use — always review each provider's terms before connecting any account.
