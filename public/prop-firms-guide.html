What is a futures prop firm?

A proprietary ("prop") trading firm lets a trader use the firm's capital instead of their own. Rather than wiring tens of thousands of dollars into a personal brokerage account, a trader pays a smaller one-time or monthly fee to attempt an evaluation — a simulated account with a profit target and a set of risk rules. Clear it, and the firm moves the trader into a funded account, still usually running in a simulated ("sim-funded") environment, where profit earned inside the rules can be requested as a real cash payout, split between trader and firm.

Why this matters: a "50K account" is not $50,000 of money a trader can lose. It's a risk budget — usually a $2,000 drawdown — layered under an evaluation fee. The account size sets the profit target and position limits; the drawdown sets the real stakes.
Evaluation / Combine

The paid trial account. Hit a profit target under a rule set to move to funded status.

Drawdown / MLL

The maximum loss allowed before the account fails. Can be trailing (rises with profit) or fixed to end-of-day equity.

Daily Loss Limit

A per-day stop. Some firms just pause trading for the session; others fail the account outright.

Consistency rule

Caps how much of total profit can come from a single best day — usually 40–50%.

Profit split

How approved payout profit is divided — from 80/20 up to 100% to the trader.

Payout cap / buffer

A cap limits the max per request; a buffer is profit that must stay in the account before anything is withdrawable.

Before you buy

Questions to answer before purchasing

?Is the drawdown calculated intraday or end-of-day, and when does it stop trailing?
?Does hitting the Daily Loss Limit pause the day, or close the account permanently?
?Is the payout amount shown before or after the profit split?
?Does the first payout lock the drawdown close to the starting balance?
?Are trade copiers, automation, news trading, and correlated positions allowed on your exact plan?
?How many sim payouts are allowed before transition, review, or account closure?
?Are current discounts changing activation fees, payout caps, or reset terms?
Risk reminder: futures trading and prop-firm evaluations involve substantial risk. Most evaluation accounts are simulated, rules are typically enforced automatically, and paying a fee does not guarantee funding or payouts.
